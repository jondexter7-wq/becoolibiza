Qualified

Why F-Gas certification matters

Air conditioning systems contain refrigerant gases that are regulated across Europe. Handling them, whether installing, re-gassing or removing a unit, requires specific training and certification.

We are F-Gas certified and City & Guilds trained, so the refrigerant side of your system is handled properly, leak-checked and documented. It's also why our installations last: correct pipe sizing, vacuuming and pressure testing are what separate a system that runs reliably for many years from one that fails in its second summer.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often should air conditioning be serviced in Ibiza?

At least once a year, ideally in spring before the heat arrives. Holiday rentals and units that run all summer benefit from a second check mid-season, as salt air, dust and heavy use clog filters and coils quickly.

How much does AC installation cost in Ibiza?

It depends on the number of rooms, the type of system and the length of the pipe runs. Send us photos or plans on WhatsApp and we'll give you a fixed written quote, usually the same day.

Which brands do you work with?

We install and service all major brands, including Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, LG, Samsung and Toshiba, and can recommend the right unit for your property and budget.

My AC isn't cooling. What should I check first?

Check it's in cool mode (not fan or dry), clean the filters, check the remote batteries and make sure the breaker hasn't tripped. If it still isn't cooling, message us: it may be low on refrigerant or have a component fault.

Can you service my AC while I'm abroad?

Yes. We arrange access with you, your villa manager or key holder, carry out the work and send you a photo report and summary the same day.

Do you install ducted air conditioning in villas?

Yes. Concealed ducted systems are ideal for villas because only discreet grilles are visible. We plan these carefully, ideally during a renovation, and can work alongside your builder or architect.
