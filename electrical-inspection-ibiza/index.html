Checklist

What we check

  • Fuse box (cuadro) condition, labelling and protection
  • RCD (diferencial) trip tests
  • Earthing and bonding
  • Visible wiring, sockets and switches
  • Outdoor, garden and pool circuits
  • Signs of overheating, damp or DIY work
  • Supply capacity versus your planned use (AC, pool, kitchen)
  • Air conditioning units (optional)
Your report

Our report, and what it isn't

You receive a written report with photos, a clear summary of priorities and fixed prices for any recommended work, in English or Spanish.

Please note: this is an independent condition report to help you make decisions. It is not the official Spanish electrical installation certificate (boletín / CIE) or a regulatory OCA inspection. If you need those for a sale, a new supply or a licence, ask us and we'll explain the process.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why get an electrical inspection before buying in Ibiza?

Older fincas and apartments often have outdated wiring, undersized fuse boxes or unsafe DIY additions. A rewire can be a significant cost, and knowing before you buy lets you budget or negotiate.

How long does an inspection take?

Most apartments and villas can be inspected in a single visit. Large fincas with outbuildings and pools take longer, and we'll tell you when you book.

Is the report in English?

Yes. Reports are available in English or Spanish, so you, your lawyer and your agent can all understand them.

Is this the same as a boletín?

No. Our report is an independent assessment of condition and safety. The boletín (CIE) is an official certificate for an installation; if you need one, we'll explain what's involved.
