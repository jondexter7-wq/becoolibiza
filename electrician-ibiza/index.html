Common question

Why does my electricity keep going off?

It's the question we hear most in Ibiza, and there are usually three culprits:

The RCD (diferencial) trips. Current is leaking to earth somewhere. Common causes are damp outdoor lights or sockets, a faulty appliance (often a water heater, washing machine or pool pump) or damaged cable. Unplug everything, reset it, then plug items back in one by one. If one trips it, you've found the culprit.

Everything goes off when you use several appliances. Your contracted power (potencia contratada) may be too low for the AC, oven and kettle running at once, and smart meters cut the supply when you exceed it. We can check your usage and advise whether to increase it with your supplier.

One circuit keeps tripping. A breaker that trips on its own usually means an overloaded circuit or a short. That needs testing, not just resetting.

Not sure which it is? Send us a photo of your fuse box on WhatsApp and we'll talk you through it.
