Safety first

What to do right now

  1. If you see smoke, flames or sparking, switch off the main switch (interruptor general) at the fuse box if it's safe to reach, and call 112 if there is any fire.
  2. If the whole street is dark, it's probably a supply outage. Contact your electricity supplier's breakdown line (usually Endesa) first.
  3. If only your property is off, check whether the main switch or the RCD (diferencial) has tripped. Try resetting it once.
  4. If it trips again straight away, leave it off and message us with a photo of the fuse box.
  5. Don't touch damp sockets, damaged cables or anything that's hot or buzzing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer a 24-hour emergency service?

We prioritise genuine emergencies 7 days a week, including Sundays. Message us on WhatsApp at any time and we'll respond as quickly as possible.

What counts as an electrical emergency?

Anything unsafe (smoke, burning smells, sparking, exposed cables), a total loss of power, or a failure that puts guests, food stock or the property at risk. If you're unsure, message us and we'll tell you.

My power keeps tripping. Is that an emergency?

If the RCD trips once and resets, it's usually not urgent. If it won't reset or trips repeatedly, there's a fault that needs testing. Message us a photo of your fuse box.

Can you help if I'm not at the property?

Yes. We regularly attend for owners abroad, working with villa managers and key holders, and send a photo report once the property is safe.
