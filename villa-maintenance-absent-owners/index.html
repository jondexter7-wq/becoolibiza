Checklist

Our pre-season checklist

  • Test and clean every AC unit and check cooling performance
  • Clear AC drains to prevent leaks inside the villa
  • Test RCDs and inspect the fuse box
  • Check outdoor sockets and lights for damp or damage
  • Test pool, garden and terrace lighting
  • Check water heater, pool pump and gate electrics
  • Report anything needing attention, with photos and a fixed price

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be in Ibiza for you to do the work?

No. Most of our absent-owner work is arranged entirely over WhatsApp. We coordinate access with your manager or key holder and send you a photo report when we've finished.

When should I book pre-season checks?

Ideally between March and May, before the summer rush and before your first guests. Slots fill up quickly as the season approaches.

How do I pay?

You receive a fixed quote before the work and an invoice afterwards, payable by bank transfer, so you don't need to be on the island.

Can you work with my existing villa manager?

Absolutely. Many of our clients have a manager or cleaner who provides access, and we keep both of you informed throughout.
